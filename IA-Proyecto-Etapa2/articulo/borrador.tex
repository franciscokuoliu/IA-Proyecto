\begin{abstract}
We present a controlled benchmark of eight models for six-class emotion recognition on a leakage-controlled version of a CARER-derived corpus. All models train on the same 32,000-text subset and are scored once on the same 37,153 test texts: a TF-IDF baseline, a BiLSTM with GloVe, two fully fine-tuned encoders (DistilBERT, BERT-base), and four decoders adapted with QLoRA (Qwen2.5-0.5B, Qwen2.5-1.5B, Gemma 4 E2B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B). BERT-base is the best model (Macro-F1 $0.9895 \pm 0.0005$ over three seeds), and the top five models lie within $0.0021$ of each other. A paired bootstrap finds a systematic $0.034$ gap between Qwen2.5-1.5B and Gemma 4 E2B and no consistent difference between Qwen2.5-1.5B and its reasoning-distilled counterpart.
